How scoring works

The score (0–100)

Each laptop gets a score for what you picked. Everyday, Study and Travel use the overall score: measured CPU and GPU benchmarks (PassMark and Notebookcheck) combined with RAM, display and build quality. Gaming weights the GPU most heavily. Creative & dev uses a workstation score built only from CPU, GPU and RAM.

Value

Value is score per riyal, with a fixed allowance so cheap machines don't win on price alone. Best value sorts by it.

The verdict

  • Great dealat least 20% under the typical price of its 25 closest spec peers, or 1.5× their value.
  • Sweet spota point on the price/score frontier where paying more buys much less extra score.
  • Good price8–20% under typical.
  • Fairwithin the normal range.
  • Priceyover 12% above typical.

Same laptop, several stores

Identical configurations are folded into one entry. The price shown is the cheapest in-stock offer, and every store is listed in the details.
